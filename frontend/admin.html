🔥 Priority areas — where to focus outreach

Areas ranked by a heat-vulnerability score (heat index, % elderly, % low-income, % without AC) combined with how far the nearest open site is. An area with no open resource nearby ranks above an equally vulnerable one with a cooling center down the street. This is a transparent, rule-based score today — see backend/src/services/riskScore.js for the plan to replace it with a trained model once real outcome data exists.

Click "Load priority areas" to rank areas by need.

🎟️ Priority access — invites & resident queue

Residents join through a single-use invite code your organization hands out. Once registered they're scored on age, income relative to the federal poverty level, employment, and insurance status, then ranked here so outreach reaches the highest-need people first. The rubric is documented in docs/NEED-SCORING.md. Exact incomes are never shown — only a band.

Click "Load resident queue" to see registered residents by priority.
